## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the quiet, monolithic prestige of ultra-premium natural extraction, deep oceanic trenches, and architectural mineral purity. Inspired by cinematic luxury interactive studios, the visual experience avoids retail clichés and tech-startup tropes, establishing an editorial presence that feels subterranean, glacial, and uncompromisingly precise.

### Emotional Demeanor
- **Stillness & Mass:** Interface elements carry visual weight without ornamentation. Negative space is expansive, treating viewports like gallery rooms rather than information terminals.
- **Natural Depth:** The atmosphere reflects deep-sea hydrostatic pressure and dark igneous slate, balanced by crystalline mineral clarity and light refraction.
- **Editorial Poise:** Copy is sparing, highly curated, and typeset with surgical kerning. Interactions mimic natural fluid viscosity and low-friction oceanic glide rather than bouncy spring mechanics.

### Core Style Philosophy
The design language combines **Architectural Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Layering**:
- Deep slate monochromatic foundations punctuated by pale glacial reflections.
- Structural layout lines with micro-opacity (hairlines that emerge and vanish like oceanic thermoclines).
- Restrained, tactile macro-surfaces that prioritize high-resolution product photography, elemental motion, and vast spatial breathing room.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around deep oceanic bathymetry and raw mineral lithology. Bright saturations are forbidden; every accent is tempered with mist, salt, and graphite undertones.

### Foundational Canvas & Depth
- **Abyss Surface (Canvas Primary):** `#0a0e14` — Deepest oceanic floor, baseline body background.
- **Trench Surface (Canvas Elevated):** `#0f1722` — Deep graphite-tinted ocean slate for modules, sections, and backdrops.
- **Shelf Surface (Container Base):** `#151d28` — Secondary paneling, card backs, and modal containers.
- **Basalt Rim (Subtle Dividers):** `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.08)` — Ultrathin mineral demarcation line.

### Luminescence & Atmospheric Accents
- **Glacial Core (Primary Accent):** `#38bdf8` — Used with extreme restraint for focal metadata, progress trackers, and precise navigational indicators. Never as solid large-area fills.
- **Misty Spray (Secondary Accent):** `#7dd3fc` — Desaturated glacial hue reserved for hover states, active cursor flares, and illuminated mineral details.
- **Mineral Sediment (Tertiary):** `#94a3b8` — Muted sea-spray slate for supporting specs, micro-labels, and tertiary context.

### Typographic Contrast
- **Sea Salt (Text Primary):** `#f8fafc` — Crisp, unpolluted calcium mist for headlines and essential reading.
- **Brine Slate (Text Secondary):** `#cbd5e1` — Balanced mineral gray for descriptions and continuous narrative blocks.
- **Deep Silt (Text Muted):** `#64748b` — Restrained slate for legal, indexing codes, and water composition tables.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial atmosphere that feels technical yet rarefied. 

- **Display & Headings:** `Space Grotesk` introduces cold geometric tension and architectural precision. Set with tight tracking on hero compositions, it gives product names and mineral indices the aura of institutional monographs.
- **Body & Continuous Reading:** `Hanken Grotesk` brings razor-sharp contemporary legibility without organic sentimentality. Generous line heights (`1.6`–`1.65`) allow paragraphs to breathe over dark oceanic canvases.
- **Micro-Copy & Indices:** Small uppercase labels (`label-caps`, `label-code`) utilize expanded tracking (`+0.14em` to `+0.2em`) to ground the layout with scientific authority, resembling cartographic coordinates and chemical batch designations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around deliberate pacing, unhurried progression, and cinematic balance designed for smooth inertial scrolling (e.g., Lenis).

### Grid Structure
- **Desktop (12-Column Editorial Grid):** 12 columns with a fixed `4rem` outer boundary margin and `2rem` fluid gutters. Text rarely occupies more than 5 columns, leaving expansive voids for full-bleed imagery, mineral macro-shots, and water flow visualizations.
- **Tablet (8-Column Grid):** 8 columns with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Elements collapse into asymmetric pairings rather than stacked blocks.
- **Mobile (4-Column Grid):** 4 columns with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Headlines retain dramatic presence through dedicated mobile display definitions.

### Vertical Rhythms & Pacing
- **Section Breaks:** Utilize `space-2xl` (8rem to 12rem on desktop) between thematic shifts. The screen is rarely crowded; each section commands its own complete viewport envelope.
- **Information Density:** Low density, high scannability. Content clusters adhere to strict mathematical alignments, anchoring elements to horizontal hairlines rather than floating arbitrarily.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids traditional drop shadows and skeuomorphic bevels. Instead, elevation is expressed through oceanic opacity, atmospheric absorption, and crisp micro-borders.

### Depth Mechanisms
1. **Atmospheric Stratification:**
   - **Base Floor (`#0a0e14`):** The void backdrop for infinite negative space.
   - **Tectonic Mid-Layer (`#0f1722` at `0.85` opacity with `24px` backdrop blur):** Navigational ribbons, floating controls, and overlay panels.
   - **Crystalline Surface (`#151d28` at `0.9` opacity with `40px` backdrop blur):** Contextual overlays, modal inspector drawers, and active water analysis views.

2. **Crystalline Hairlines (Ghost Borders):**
   - Structural depth is defined by 1px solid borders set to `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.08)` on rest and `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)` on focus or activation.
   - Borders do not encase every element; they are frequently applied as single top or bottom horizontal guide wires.

3. **Ambient Glacial Glow:**
   - Where light is cast, it appears as an ultra-diffuse radial field: `radial-gradient(circle at top, rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.04) 0%, transparent 70%)`. No harsh drop shadows or artificial box-casts exist within the system.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an uncompromising **Sharp (`roundedness: 0`)** architectural geometry. 

- **Form Rationale:** Natural crystals, tectonic plates, and high-end editorial layouts gain authority from crisp edges and clean right angles. Soft, bubbly radii contradict the mineral, monolithic identity.
- **Exceptions:** Pure geometric circles are permitted only for functional indicators, circular aperture iconography, or technical coordinate reticles. All buttons, cards, dialogs, media framing, and containers maintain razor-sharp `0px` corners.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Monolith):** Solid Sea Salt (`#f8fafc`) background with deep slate (`#0a0e14`) Space Grotesk uppercase typography. Sharp corners. On hover, background shifts cleanly to Glacial Tint (`#7dd3fc`) with zero transition bounce.
- **Secondary Action (Submerged Outline):** Transparent fill, 1px border `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.15)`, text in Sea Salt. On hover, border illuminates to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.5)` with background washing to `rgba(21, 29, 40, 0.6)`.
- **Text / Editorial Action:** Monospace uppercase index (`label-caps`) paired with an em-dash or hairline directional arrow. Text transitions from `Brine Slate` to `Glacial Core` on cursor glide.

### Cards & Modular Containers
- Framed by 1px hairlines (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.08)`).
- Interior fill utilizes Trench Surface (`#0f1722`) with no ambient box-shadow.
- Padding uses `space-lg` internally. Content is anchored with mineral metadata tags in top corners, preserving cinematic asymmetry.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Flat horizontal-border style: background is either transparent or `rgba(15, 23, 34, 0.4)`.
- Resting state displays a singular bottom hairline in `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.15)`.
- Active / focused state illuminates the baseline to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.8)` with typography in pure Sea Salt (`#f8fafc`). Placeholder text sits in `Deep Silt` (`#64748b`).

### Checkboxes & Binary Selectors
- Sharp 14x14px square markers with 1px border `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.2)`.
- When checked, the box is filled with `#38bdf8` and inset with a 2px inner border of `#0a0e14`, creating a precision instrument cross-check rather than an illustrated tick mark.

### Data Chips & Mineral Badges
- Ultra-compact rectangular capsules (sharp corners).
- Background: `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.04)`. Border: 1px `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.1)`.
- Typography: `label-code`, uppercase, tracking `+0.14em`, rendered in `Tertiary Slate` (`#94a3b8`).

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Bathymetric Depth Scrubber:** An ultrathin, vertical or horizontal coordinate timeline marking source extraction depth (e.g., `-1,200m`), TDS mineral levels, and harvesting coordinates.
- **Spectral Mineral Table:** Technical, tabular alignment with alternating micro-opacity rows displaying calcium, silica, and magnesium concentrations with high decimal precision.
